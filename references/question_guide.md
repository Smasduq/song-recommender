# Question Guide

This guide provides example wording for asking users about their music preferences. The agent should pick natural, conversational phrasing that fits the context.

## Core Questions

### 1. Mood or occasion
Good phrasing:
- "What's your mood or what are you listening for?"
- "Are you looking for something for a specific situation - like working out, studying, relaxing, or something else?"
- "What's the vibe you're going for?"

### 2. Favorite artists or songs
Good phrasing:
- "What are 2–3 artists or songs you already love?"
- "Who are some of your go-to artists right now?"
- "Name a couple of songs you never skip."

### 3. Preferences and dislikes
Good phrasing:
- "Any genres, languages, or eras you're into? Anything you want to avoid?"
- "What kind of music do you usually gravitate toward?"
- "Is there anything you're not in the mood for?"

### 4. Who it's for
Good phrasing:
- "Are these recommendations for you, or someone else?"
- "If it's for someone else, how would you describe their taste?"

## Optional Extras

### Energy level
- "Do you want something calm, somewhere in the middle, or high energy?"
- "How energetic should it be?"

### Content preferences
- "Does it matter if the lyrics are clean or explicit?"
- "Any content preferences to be aware of?"

### Discovery vs familiarity
- "Do you want something familiar, or something new to you?"
- "Are you looking to discover new music, or stick with what you know?"

## Tips

- Ask everything in one short, natural message when possible
- Skip questions that are already answered in the user's request
- Don't overcomplicate - taste and mood are usually enough
- Adapt phrasing to match the user's tone
